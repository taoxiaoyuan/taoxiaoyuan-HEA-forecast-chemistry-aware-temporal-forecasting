# Data licensing and redistribution

## Releaseable project data

The authors' derived factual annotations, canonical element-set identifiers,
reaction labels, temporal split definitions, numeric features, model outputs,
statistics, and figure source tables may be released under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).

## Third-party content

OpenAlex identifiers, DOI strings, titles, dates, venue names, and retrieval
metadata are provided as provenance pointers. Abstract text and publisher PDFs
are not included in the public archive unless their individual licences permit
redistribution. Users should retrieve those records from OpenAlex or the
publisher under the applicable terms.
