# Forward prediction portfolio

The portfolio was frozen on 16 October 2026 for monitoring from 1 January 2027
through 31 December 2028.

- `forward_candidate_audit_all280.csv`: inclusion/exclusion accounting for all 280 frozen candidates.
- `forward_eligible_top30.csv`: highest-ranked candidates after conservative evidence and scope screens.
- `forward_portfolio_2027_2028.csv`: 12 reaction-balanced, testable directions.
- `forward_selection_flow.csv`: counts at each selection stage.
- `forward_freeze_manifest_20261016.json`: freeze rule, scope, and interpretation boundary.

The hypotheses are prospective outputs, not experimental validation. A relation
not found in the documented searches may still exist outside the retrieved
corpora or under different terminology.
